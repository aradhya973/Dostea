01

About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies when you use the DOSTEA website, create a customer account, place an order, reserve a table or contact our team. It should be read together with DOSTEA's Terms and Conditions.

DOSTEA is responsible for deciding why and how personal information is used for these services. By providing information, you confirm that it is accurate and that you are authorised to provide it.

02

Information we may collect

The information collected depends on the service you use:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number and account identifiers.
  • Order information: selected items, quantities, price, payment status and order history.
  • Reservation information: date, time, guest count, preferences and booking notes.
  • Support information: messages, feedback and details you submit through contact forms.
  • Technical information: browser, device, website activity, log data and approximate network information where available.
03

How we use information

DOSTEA may use personal information to:

  • Create, secure and manage customer accounts.
  • Accept, prepare, process and track food orders.
  • Manage table reservations and service requests.
  • Send essential confirmations, updates and support replies.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse and security incidents.
  • Improve website performance, menu availability and customer experience.
  • Meet legal, accounting or regulatory obligations.

Promotional messages should be sent only when you have chosen to receive them or when otherwise permitted. You can ask to stop such messages at any time.

04

Cookies and browser storage

The website may use cookies or similar browser storage to keep you signed in, remember cart items, retain preferences, improve security and understand website performance. Some storage is necessary for requested features to work.

You may control cookies through your browser. Blocking essential cookies or clearing browser storage may sign you out or remove saved cart and preference information.

05

Payment information

Online payments may be handled by an independent payment service provider. DOSTEA may receive payment status, transaction reference, amount and limited billing details required to confirm or refund an order. Full card, bank or UPI credentials should be entered only on the payment provider's secure payment interface.

06

When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary with:

  • Hosting, database, authentication, payment and communication providers supporting the website.
  • DOSTEA staff or authorised service partners who need it to fulfil an order, reservation or support request.
  • Professional advisers, authorities or other parties when required by law or needed to protect legal rights and safety.
  • A successor organisation if DOSTEA is restructured or transferred, subject to appropriate safeguards.
07

How long information is kept

Personal information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including account management, customer support, fraud prevention and legal, tax or accounting requirements. Information may then be securely deleted or anonymised unless longer retention is required by law.

08

Information security

DOSTEA uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect customer information. However, no website, network or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password confidential and notify us if you suspect unauthorised activity on your account.

09

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and reasonable verification, you may ask DOSTEA to provide information about your personal data, correct inaccurate details, update incomplete information, delete eligible information or address a privacy grievance.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing already completed and may limit features that require the information concerned.

Access & correction Review or correct eligible account information.
Deletion & consent Request eligible deletion or withdraw consent.
10

Children's privacy

If applicable law requires a parent or lawful guardian to consent to a child's use of an online service, the account or service must be used only with that consent. A parent or guardian may contact DOSTEA about personal information submitted by a child under their care.

DOSTEA does not knowingly use children's information for targeted advertising or behavioural monitoring.

11

Third-party services and links

The website may connect to third-party services such as sign-in, mapping or payment providers. Their privacy policies apply when you interact directly with them. DOSTEA is not responsible for the privacy practices of an unrelated third-party website.

Some service providers may process information from locations outside your state or country. Where applicable, DOSTEA expects providers to use appropriate safeguards and follow relevant law.

12

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when DOSTEA services, technology or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. A prominent notice may be provided when a change materially affects how information is used.

13

PRIVACY QUESTION OR REQUEST?

Contact DOSTEA.

To ask a privacy question, exercise an available data right or submit a grievance, contact the DOSTEA team through our support page. Include enough information for us to understand and verify the request.
